# PSX Vehicles Free

**12 free PS1-style props for a street**: a sedan whose doors, hood and trunk open and whose wheels roll and steer, its rusted wreck on flat tires, a pickup, a bicycle whose cranks turn, and the road, kerb, fence, cones, tires and hydrant to stand them in. Low-poly and point-filtered, a scene per prop, ready to drop in. Free for commercial use.

### Want more?

**[PSX Vehicles](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-vehicles)** has 43 vehicles and 27 street pieces
in 278 animations: sedans, a police car whose beacons turn, a taxi, coupes, hatchbacks, wagons and
a hearse, pickups, vans, an ambulance, an ice cream van, a school bus and a city bus, motorcycles
and a moped, their wrecks, and a junkyard, parking bays and a garage door, with a Unity package.
Same addon, same look: it installs over this one. The night sky over the street is one of
[PSX Skyboxes](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-skyboxes)' 26.

### What's inside

- **Cars:** a blue sedan and a rusted one on flat tires, broken glass and torn seats, whose four
  doors, hood and trunk open.
- **Pickup:** a red pickup whose doors, hood and tailgate open.
- **Bicycle:** its wheels and cranks turn and its bars steer.
- **Street:** a 6 by 4 m piece of road, pavement and kerb, a corrugated fence, a traffic cone, a
  hydrant, a jerry can, and tires loose and stacked.
- **25 animations** on the moving parts: each door, the hood and the trunk alone or all at once,
  and the wheels roll and steer. The parts are separate objects on their hinges and axles, so your
  code can drive them too.
- **12 icons**, 256x256 PNGs with transparency, rendered from the models.
- 8 to 1,652 triangles a prop, one 1024x1024 texture atlas, shading baked into the vertex colours,
  real-world scale.

### Godot 4.3 to 4.7

An addon with a scene per prop: box colliders on every part (the moving ones move with them), an
AnimationPlayer where it moves, one lit material so your headlights and flashlight fall on them,
and a gallery. Tested on Godot 4.3 and 4.7, on Forward+, Mobile and Compatibility.

```gdscript
# sedan_blue: the prop in your level
sedan_blue.get_node("AnimationPlayer").play("open_front_left")
```

For the full PS1 look, [PSX Look](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-look) turns the props (and your
level) over in one line: wobbling vertices, warping textures, 240p, dither and 15-bit colour. Its
[free sampler](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-look-free) has the vertex snap and affine textures.
